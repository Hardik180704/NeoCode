# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for
everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and patient.
- Focus feedback on ideas and code, not people.
- Accept responsibility, apologize when appropriate, and learn from mistakes.
- Respect privacy and never publish another person's credentials or personal data.
- Help make participation safe and productive for newcomers.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks,
trolling, deliberate disruption, doxxing, or publishing private information are
not tolerated in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or other content
that violates this code. They may issue a warning, temporary restriction, or
permanent ban depending on severity and repeated behavior.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact
information on their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/Hardik180704). Do not
open a public issue containing sensitive personal information. Reports will be
reviewed as confidentially and promptly as reasonably possible.

This code applies in repository spaces and whenever someone is representing the
project in public.
